EQ show: production playbook (Ep3 onward)

Written 28 Oct 2026 (AEDT), after Blake approved the Ep2 Kling cut ("take stock of the fixes so we know how to create the next"). Sources: VERIFICATION.md issue log; spend.jsonl for Ep1 (/workspace/eq-pepe-ep1-dip) and Ep2 (/workspace/eq-ep2-spub); SCRIPT.md v1/v2/v3; build scripts (kling_jobs.py, edit_kling.py, cue_kling.py, phonequad.py, houghkeys.py, keytrack.py, medtrack.py); review pages.

How to use it: work top to bottom. Every lesson sits at the stage where it is cheapest to apply, BEFORE money is spent. VERIFICATION.md stays the after-the-fact gate and issue log. Each rule here has a matching check there.

1. Script (free: get it right here)

Structure (target 45–55 s; Ep2 landed at 52.1 s, Ep1 ran to 62–63 s against a 56 s target):

Beat Time What
Hook 0–5 s A motion-led arrival or visual gag plus one announcer line. Name the setting in the first line.
HIGH ~5–20 s EQ arrives warm and confident. Greet the crew. Plant ONE visual setup that the ending will pay off (Ep2: Larry's chain reads "≠").
LOW ~20–35 s Someone (usually Larry) is wrong or in trouble. The worried-face reaction shots live here.
Twist ~30–40 s A researched, on-brand reveal (Ep2: the ladies are the Sonic Pub bribe desk).
Payoff ~40–44 s ONE payoff of the core joke, paying off the setup from the front half (Ep2: EQ flicks the slash off ≠ → "There. Now you're equal."). It is the last character beat.
Celebration ~44–47 s ONE celebration (Ep2: a single WAGMI cheer).
End card + tag ~47–52 s ONE short tag (≤3 s) that calls back to an early gag and does not repeat the punchline word. The loop's last frame blends into the first.

Rules learned:

  • No double-ups after the twist. Ep2 v1 stacked four "equal" payoffs (pool split, equal pours, "I'm EQUAL!", "Where everybody's equal") plus two celebrations. Blake: "a fair bit of double up".

    • Count "equal" / "=" payoffs after the twist: at most one spoken.
    • The tag must not repeat the payoff word or the cold-open line ("another week in the trenches" said twice), unless it is the single deliberate callback.

  • Time every TTS line before writing the shot list. Render the lines, measure them, and let the dialogue length set the floor of each shot.

    • Gap between consecutive lines ≥ the previous line's end + 0.3 s, or the subtitles overlap (Ep2 S17).
    • Keep tags ≤3 s. Ep2's first tag ran 3.8 s and repeated "Friday nights".
    • Run volumedetect on every trimmed VO file. One ffmpeg typo muted a whole line at −91 dB.

  • Decide the clinch and its setup at script stage, and note which prop changes state (≠ → =). Every later shot must show the new state.

  • Avoid symbolic hand poses (an "=" handshake isn't achievable with these models). Do symbols as graphics.
  • One speaker per lip-sync shot wherever possible. Group lines (WAGMI) go on wide shots that don't need sync.
  • Plan reaction shots up front: each LOW beat needs a worried or sweating still of that character.

Naming: EQ (never Pepe). "Sonic Pub" in lines, captions, end cards, phone headers and signs. SPUB only as a ticker. Before publishing, grep SCRIPT.md, page builders, SPEAK/captions and lines.tsv for stale names ("S Pub", "Ess Pub", "Pepe").

Trenches lingo bank (researched in /workspace/eq-ep2-spub/RESEARCH.md, used naturally, never more than about one per line):

  • gm / gm gm · ser · rolling in (hot) · heavy bags / bags · few understand
  • WAGMI / NGMI · send it / full send · up only · LFG
  • jeets / paper hands · we're so back / it's so over · collab / 🤝 · bought the dip
  • "another week in the trenches" (cold-open only) · "we are all $EQUAL" (community line; use sparingly as the payoff)
  • Keep "wife-changing money / poors will be minted" off-script, because it edges toward money claims.

Script exit gate:

  • ☐ Hook in ≤5 s.
  • ☐ One payoff, one celebration and one tag.
  • ☐ Count of "equal" after the twist ≤1, and the tag is free of it.
  • ☐ Every line rendered and timed; total 45–55 s.
  • ☐ Each lip-sync shot has one clear speaker.
  • ☐ Prop state changes listed.
  • ☐ No numbers or claims.
  • ☐ Names grep clean.

2. Plates and stills (free: most paid failures were already visible here)

Generate plates with GenerateImage, then gate EVERY plate before any paid motion. Kling and Wan carry the plate's faults straight into the clip.

Composition

  • Subject large in frame: a speaking or acting character fills ≥40% of frame height. Zooming more than 1.3× on 1080×1920 goes soft.

    • Ep1 S12 Rekula and Ep2 S01 EQ were too small.
    • Fix the plate, not the zoom.

  • Arrival and action plates: frame tight enough that the action finishes inside the shot (Ep2 p01k re-plate: EQ about 2× larger, so the step-out completes in ~1.7 s).

  • Group shots: fill the background. Empty space invites the model to invent new characters (Ep2 M4 added two extra frogs).

Expression and identity

  • EQ: eyes open and round with a warm open grin. Never half-lidded, sly or smug. The default "Pepe eyes" read smug.

    • Regenerate the plate (it's free) for every EQ hero and clinch shot.
    • Ep2 needed new p02w, p16w, p20w and p20x after K02 and K15 were wasted on half-lidded plates.

  • Worried-face stills ready for every reaction shot (Larry sweating or wincing, e.g. p08w). Expressions are much more reliable from the plate than from the prompt.

  • Identity check against the canonical still for each character. Ep1 p21 Hedgy already had a cat mouth and whiskers.

Props

  • Medallion: burn the E onto EQ's disc on the plate (plates/logo/). Give Larry's disc its episode state.
  • Phones: held with a blank white or green screen, upright and not over-tilted, with fingers clearly separated from the screen. The UI is composited later (§5). Never ask the model to draw a phone UI.

Text, signs and badges

  • Clean neon and signage: scan every plate for gibberish text ("SOAYCHN", "SOYCHN", "LAMBO BROS", blackboard scrawl) and paint it out or composite the real sign.

    • Use ONE sign layout (stacked "SONIC PUB" board, aspect 1.9) on every plate, so the clips match.
    • Size sign-cover boxes to include ALL parts of the old sign (mug, frame).

  • Badge-free cars and clothes: check the hood, grille and lapels. Paint out pins and emblems on the still.

  • Derived plates (a frame grabbed from a Kling or Wan clip to seed the next shot) get the full gate again. Ep1 p02x carried Kling's lapel pin and nose badge.

Plate exit gate (zoom-check each plate at 100%):

  • ☐ On-model look.
  • ☐ Eyes open with a warm grin.
  • ☐ E on the medallion.
  • ☐ No glasses, pins or badges.
  • ☐ No gibberish text.
  • ☐ Subject ≥40% height.
  • ☐ Phone screen blank.
  • ☐ Group background filled.
  • ☐ Correct prop state.

3. Kling 2.6 Pro prompting (image-to-video, 5 s, 9:16, 24 fps, 121 frames)

Use the prompt blocks from /workspace/eq-ep2-spub/build/kling_jobs.py (copy the file per episode):

  • STY: "2D cartoon cel animation with thick dark outlines and flat colours, continuing exactly from the start frame, same characters and outfits."
  • EQ lock (any shot with EQ): on-model list, "exactly ONE gold chain with one round medallion, NO glasses, NO sunglasses, a big warm open-mouthed beaming grin with eyes wide open and round the whole time (never squinting, never smirking, never sly or smug, no raised eyebrow)".
  • END: "Keep every round medallion and disc exactly where it is. No new badges, pins or emblems on clothes or cars. No text, no letters, no new logos."
  • LOCKCAR for car shots: "The roadster has no badges, no emblems, no logos, no text."
  • NEG: morphing, smirk, sly grin, squinting eyes, half-closed eyes, patch, pocket square, face change, extra limbs or fingers, extra characters, text, letters, subtitles, watermark, logo distortion, sunglasses or glasses on the frog, lapel pin, badge, brooch, car emblem, extra chains, scene change, camera cut, reverse motion, fire, flames.
  • Always add "Static camera, no zoom."

Prompt craft that worked:

  • One clear physical action per shot, with contact made explicit ("their hands clearly meet and slap").
  • Put the beat "within the first second" when timing matters.
  • Name characters by look ("the orange gecko with gold chains"), not by name.
  • Plan to use frame windows: most good clips give 1.5–3 s of clean frames. Window them per shot (e.g. K06b f18–66, K10 f14+).

Kling exit gate:

  • ☐ Strip at 0/25/50/75/100% plus a scrub.
  • ☐ Look, E and expression held across the window.
  • ☐ Action happens and runs the right direction.
  • ☐ No invented props or text.
  • ☐ No flames.
  • ☐ Clean window ≥ shot length at native speed.

4. Lip-sync (Wan 2.7: 720×1280, 30 fps, 150 frames, audio-driven)

Which shots qualify:

  • ONE clear speaking face, ≥40% of frame height, front or three-quarter view, mouth unobstructed.
  • Body motion small. Wan can carry a simple action (Ep2 W05c hug mid-line, W14 open hand).

  • Don't sync:

    • Multi-speaker group shots (Ep2 S16: five small faces, and Wan drives one face and would lose the Kling clink choreography).
    • Off-screen lines.
    • Fast action shots where the Kling motion is the point.

  • Write these into the script as deliberate wide or off-screen moments.

Drive audio:

  • Use lead seconds of silence plus the line wav, padded to 5 s. Shot mapping: wan_frame = (t − offset + lead) × 30.
  • Two lines from the same speaker can share one drive (Ep2 W03 = L03 + L05). Leave other speakers' lines out of the drive.

Prompt: "Only speaks … mouth opens and closes in sync … then closes and rests", plus the voice mood. Add " does NOT speak: mouth stays closed" for any second face. Add the EQ lock for EQ shots, plus "Static camera, no zoom."

Re-take checks (Ep2 W05b lost the E and went sly; Ep1 W16 added tears):

  • ☐ Right character speaking.
  • ☐ Mouth in sync through the whole line.
  • ☐ E on the medallion (re-burn regardless).
  • ☐ Expression warm and wide-eyed, not sly.
  • ☐ No tears or added emotion.
  • ☐ Any action beat lands INSIDE the shot window (Ep2 W05: the hug came after the line).
  • ☐ Move SFX to the new beat (the S05 whoosh moved to 1.85 s).

5. Compositing and post

Phone UI: always corner-pinned, occluded and light-matched, never a flat overlay (Blake, Ep2 fix pass).

  1. Plate with a blank screen (§2). Track the 4 screen corners per frame with build/phonequad.py (convex hull, then a 4-point approx, then Gaussian sigma 2). Check the max step is under 1 px/frame.

  2. Warp the UI (built in screen space, 321×600) with getPerspectiveTransform. Expand the quad by 3 px under the bezel. Apply a rounded-corner mask (r≈26).

  3. Occlusion: - Fingers and thumb = non-white plate pixels inside the quad, morph-opened so the antialiased rim isn't read as a finger. - Leftover white in a 7 px rim → bezel black.

  4. Light match: - Emissive spill (blurred screen, ~0.35) onto fingers and bezel; inner bezel shade. - Faint diagonal glass sheen; 0.55 px blur plus grain matched to the plate. - Motion blur when a corner moves >2 px/frame. Use a stateless frame-to-frame delta.

  5. Gate: start, middle and end frames zoomed at 3.5× for a white rim, sliding or jitter.

Medallions and chain overlays:

  • Re-track and re-burn the E on EVERY EQ medallion in every generated clip, as a standard step.

  • Tools:

    • medtrack.py --gold.
    • holekeys.py dense keys.
    • houghkeys.py / Hough circles for walking shots.
    • keytrack.py: manual keys plus refine.

  • Never trust tpltrack confidence below 0.7. Never keep a half-faded ghost stamp: cut to raw frames or hard re-burn.

  • Fast swings: track per frame (manual keys plus a Hough refine, low smoothing). QA every source frame of the swing on a crop grid with the track circle drawn, not just output frames at 0.1 s (Ep2 S15 trailing).

  • Edge-on turns: perspective-match the overlay.

    • Track a width scale (blob w/h) and draw the coin as a foreshortened ellipse.
    • Fade only the GLYPH as it goes edge-on.

  • Never fade the cover coin when the plate's own prop is the wrong design. Ep2 S16's fade revealed Larry's old blue E.

  • Keep the model's own glint on top of the overlay with a luminance mask.
  • After any shot reorder or prop change, re-check the prop state in every later shot.

Glints and bursts:

  • Horizontal shimmer only across an "=" (a diagonal glint reads as "≠").
  • Rays start outside the coin rim. Put the "=" glyph above the medallion with a dark soft shadow, so the E stays visible and the glyph reads on a whiteout.
  • Time it to the clip's own flash (Ep2 S10, t = 1.58 s).

Lighting beats:

  • Do "lights up" moments as a graded effect (warm radial bloom plus fairy lights popping on along the rim) over clean pre-light frames.

Signs and captions:

  • Cover signs baked into clips with a per-frame tracked bbox and an opaque board sized to the whole old sign.
  • Cap caption width (≤700 px) and check against the top-left E watermark after any text change.

Speed: native speed only. If a clip is too short, re-plate or re-prompt; don't slow it down or frame-blend.

Tooling notes:

  • The Read tool caches by filename, so give QA images unique names.
  • finish_kling.sh takes an out BASE, not a .mp4 path.

6. Audio and mix spec

  • Voices: only the saved EQPepe-* ElevenLabs voices, via render_voice.py, with emotion tags in brackets ("[warm, grinning]").

    • EQ = EQPepe-CoolCat (pepe). Others: announcer, larry, hedgy, rekula, contessa, scarlett, queenie, vivienne.
    • Group cheers are stacks of 5 single-voice takes.

  • Fit: silence-trim each line into vo/fit/. Run volumedetect on every trimmed file. Check consecutive lines are ≥0.3 s apart.

  • Mix:

    • Run /workspace/eq-pepe-audio/mix.py from a cue file (cue_kling.py → cue_kling.json): dialogue −16.5 LUFS, music ducked about 10–12 dB under dialogue, SFX −6 dB under dialogue.
    • Master to −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP true peak, then AAC 192k at 48 kHz.
    • Measure the result; don't assume it (Ep2 final: −14.4 LUFS, −1.6 dBTP).

  • Sound cues:

    • Tie SFX to on-screen contact moments (high-five slaps, the bags thud, the hug whoosh, the medallion chime at the flash).
    • Re-time a cue whenever a re-take moves the action.
    • Music changes per act (arrival swing → table bed → bossa for the twist → full swing on the flick → bossa loop for the tag).

  • Loop: the last frame blends into the first (seam diff checked; Ep2 1.02).

8. Pre-flight checklist and per-stage QA gates

Before any spend:

  • ☐ Script gate (§1) passed and Blake has seen the preview.
  • ☐ Every line rendered, timed and volume-checked; shot list built to those times.
  • ☐ Every plate passed the plate gate (§2), including derived plates.
  • ☐ Each shot is tagged Kling (action), Wan (single-face dialogue) or coded/graphic (symbols, light-ups, phone UI), with the reason.
  • ☐ Prompts built from STY + EQ + END (+ LOCKCAR) and NEG. No light-up or glow near glasses, no symbolic hand poses, timing in "within N seconds".
  • ☐ Post plan per shot: which medallions need a re-burn, phones to pin, glyph overlays, graded lighting.
  • ☐ Live wallet read and logged; batch quoted; cap and reserve confirmed; retry line budgeted.
