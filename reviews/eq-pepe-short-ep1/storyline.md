## Series bible

### Pepe's personality rules
1. **Cool first, chaos second.** He never panics — he *chooses* chaos. Unbothered smirk in every frame, even mid-disaster.
2. **Always on a mission.** Every episode he gets a "ping" and goes — no hesitating, no explaining.
3. **Swagger in the small stuff.** No-look placements, bowtie adjustments, finger guns, perfect landings — and one slick move that backfires per episode.
4. **Never gets to sip.** The wine is his eternal unfinished business.
5. **He's the guide, not the shill.** He *points* people to Equalizer and Sonic; he never quotes numbers or promises gains.
6. **Speaks rarely.** Max 1–2 short lines per episode. The face does the talking.

### Recurring gags & catchphrase ideas
- The interrupted sip (cold open of every episode).
- "Hold my wine." (his mission-accept line)
- The no-look wine placement — and whatever happens to the glass while he's gone.
- Bowtie adjustment = "mission mode on".
- Too-fast-for-the-selfie gag (Sonic speed).
- Catchphrase ideas: "Liquidity lives here." · "Too fast. Love that." · "Equalize it." · "Back before the wine breathes."
- Episode titles in Bebas Neue: "EP. XX — [3–4 WORDS]".

### Visual rules
- **9:16, 1080×1920.** Safe zones: nothing important in the top ~250px or bottom ~420px (platform UI); keep key action away from the right ~130px (like/comment buttons).
- **Palette:** deep navy (#05070F–#0B1630) + electric cyan (#28D2FF), white type; small warm accents only (his red headband, the red wine, orange sparks).
- **Pepe is ALWAYS the real sticker art** (curly hair, red headband, black tux, blue bowtie, Equalizer pin, red wine) composited in code — never AI-redrawn — for on-model consistency. New poses = new hand-made sticker art from the art team, not AI.
- **All text in code** (PIL, Bebas Neue), never AI-generated. AI backgrounds must be text-free, logo-free, character-free.
- Equalizer logo small, from the brand pack only. Real dApp UI shown via actual screenshots.
- Loopable endings: last frame/sound cuts back to the first.

### Future episode ideas
1. **EP. 02 — The Wine Thief:** Pepe chases the glass-snatching silhouette through Sonic's neon back alleys — it leads him straight to a liquidity pool.
2. **The LP Pool Party:** Pepe throws a party at a pool that turns out to be a *liquidity* pool; he explains pairing two tokens with a cannonball.
3. **Bridge Run:** Pepe sprints across a neon bridge into Sonic, racing a wall of slow-loading spinners from "elsewhere".
4. **The Vote:** Pepe campaigns like a politician for a pool to get emissions in the epoch vote — kissing babies, shaking hands, finger guns.
5. **Pepe vs. the Clock:** Pepe tries to do one thing slower than a Sonic block confirms. He loses every time.
